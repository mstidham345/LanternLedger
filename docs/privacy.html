Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Short version: LanternLedger does not collect, sell, or share any information about you or your business. Your books stay on your own computer.

What LanternLedger stores, and where

Everything you enter — your business details, clients, invoices, expenses, sales, mileage, pay records, receipt pictures, logos, backups and exports — is saved only on your PC, in this folder:

%APPDATA%\LanternLedger

The same folder is used whether you installed LanternLedger from the website or from the Microsoft Store. Nothing in it is uploaded anywhere. You can copy, back up, or delete it at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete your books unless you choose to.

What LanternLedger does not do

  • No account, sign-up, or login.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or usage statistics.
  • No crash reports or diagnostics are sent anywhere.
  • No cloud storage. The author never sees, hosts, or can recover your books.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone — there is no data to share.

When LanternLedger uses the internet

  • Check for updates (website version only). If you click Check for updates in Settings, the app asks GitHub which version is newest. That request contains no books data; like any website, GitHub can see your computer’s internet address. The Microsoft Store version does not do this — the Store handles its own updates.
  • Links you click. Links such as IRS / EFTPS pages, the support email address, or Buy Me a Coffee open in your normal web browser or email program. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Otherwise the app works fully offline.

Buy Me a Coffee

Settings includes an optional Buy Me a Coffee link for anyone who wants to leave a tip. It opens the Buy Me a Coffee website in your browser. Tipping is entirely voluntary, is handled by Buy Me a Coffee (not by LanternLedger), and does not unlock or change anything in the app. LanternLedger never receives your payment details.

Microsoft Store

If you get LanternLedger from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. LanternLedger itself receives no information about you from the Store beyond anonymous, aggregate download numbers Microsoft shows to app publishers.

Email

If you choose to email for help, I will only see what you send, and I will use it only to answer you.

Children

LanternLedger is a business tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
